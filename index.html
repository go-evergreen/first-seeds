01

Plant Your Roots

Before you share a single thing, get clear on your own story.

Everything you'll share later grows from this. Not a brand script — your words. Why this, why now, why you. These three answers grow roots underground. Hit continue when you're done — that's when the seed sprouts.

YOUR STORY SO FAR — SAY IT OUT LOUD ONCE
KEEP IT CLEAN
Keep your story about your experience and your values — not what a product cured or fixed. "This is the standard I've been looking for" is yours to say. "This healed my ___" is a health claim, and those cause real problems as we head into the US launch. The checker under each box will nudge you if a word could read as a claim.

KEEP IT CLEAN · READ BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
Don’t copy the official Ringana page — or anyone else’s landing page. Yours should look and sound like you: your story, your voice, your photos where you can. The personal part is what no corporate (or borrowed) page can replicate.

Tie product talk to real data or your own honest experience. “Here’s what I noticed using it” is fine. Specific health or medical claims are not — they trip platform filters and attract regulator attention.

U.S. launch snapshot

Current guidance shared with founding partners (subject to change):

Pre-registration

Starts October 1. $0 to reserve your spot. No obligation — it secures your place and helps Ringana prepare for launch. Business Booster fee expected to be waived in October for that window.

Founder packs

Current guidance: about $220 (~110 points). Those points are set to reallocate into November when the U.S. market opens, supporting early qualification in the plan. Details can refine closer to Miami / launch.

Staying active

One of the gentlest parts: stay active with a personal order or a customer order once every 12 months. That's it.

Build where Ringana grows

You're not limited to the U.S. As eligible markets open, you can build internationally. Ringana is established across Europe; Canada is on the roadmap for 2027.

Your purchases

Partners purchase at retail and earn commission back based on the tier you've achieved — currently spoken of as 19% / 29% / 39%. Everyone shops the same way; income reflects the business you've actually built.

Online shop & codes

Partners are always in the searchable database at checkout. Optional personal shop: current fee about $10/month (first 3 months free with Business Booster). Optional personalized customer discount code: about $99. Recurring orders through your shop stay connected to you.

Why start now?

Momentum isn't created on launch day. It's created before.

Many companies wait until people join — then everything hits at once: products, ingredients, systems, tech, relationships. We're doing the opposite.

By launch, the hope is you'll already understand what makes the products unique, why freshness matters, which products fit different goals, how The Fresh Grove operates, and who your leadership is. So day one feels like opening a door to a home you've already helped build — not drinking from a fire hose.
